NOTES
- Browsers (except Safari) can't show .heic. Convert to .jpg (heictojpg.com, or Photos app export).
- Phone videos are big. Compress to ~720p (HandBrake) so it loads fast.
- Edit all text/captions in the CONFIG block at the top of script.js.
- Test locally: run "python -m http.server" in this folder, open on your phone via your PC's IP.
